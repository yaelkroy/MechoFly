# N2.1 Spatial Embodiment Correction

This bounded corrective slice follows the user-observed N2 visual defects.

## Defects

1. The contact shadow was authored in body-local coordinates and passed through
   the same heading rotation as the insect. The shadow could therefore appear
   above or beside the fly.
2. Landing used the bottom edge of the virtual screen as its target Y
   coordinate. Every completed flight therefore collapsed toward the screen
   bottom.

## Correction

- Adds explicit `altitude_pixels` as a Z coordinate independent of screen X/Y.
- Projects the shadow in screen space below the body.
- Makes shadow distance, size, and opacity monotonic in Z altitude.
- Uses bounded takeoff, cruise, and touchdown altitude envelopes.
- Replaces bottom-edge landing with a local safe-perch target.
- Preserves continuous position, heading, and altitude across refresh rates.
- Leaves N2 neural dynamics, thresholds, holds, telemetry, and visible behavior
  selection unchanged.

## Claim boundary

The Z coordinate and local landing surface are engineered presentation/motion
state. They are not a reconstructed biomechanical depth estimate or a detected
physical desktop surface. Screen Ecotope surface sensing remains a later
milestone.
