# N4.1-D uncued exploratory-flight correction

## Entry decision

The R7 R4 supplemental review rejected the frozen R6 R2 candidate even though
its original flight gate passed. Across twelve complete flights on a virtual
desktop with an inferred usable horizontal range of `8-2132 px`, the pet used
only `8-682 px`. Duration variation, speed variation, total path length, and
rapid turns therefore did not protect against repeated one-side confinement.

R6 R2 remains frozen evidence. This additive branch changes only the
feature-gated flight motor, direct review trace, and review metrics. It does not
change any parameter JSON, walk or grooming behavior, takeoff timing, landing
timing, the canonical N4 build, or deployment state.

## Biological boundary

The correction does **not** make screen coverage a motor goal. A fly's
foraging strategy depends on sensory evidence and metabolic state. Drosophila
can explore before finding food, then switch to area-restricted or local search
after food or sugar-related sensory input. An uncued desktop pet has no modeled
hunger, odor plume, taste event, or food location, so forcing it to sweep the
display would mislabel a product heuristic as food-search biology.

Free-flight work does support comparatively straight segments separated by
rapid body saccades, with visual expansion contributing to collision-avoidance
turning. The N4.1-D motor therefore uses only those qualitative motifs:

- each Flight bout samples a reproducible, position-independent exploratory
  course;
- the first rapid saccade turns toward that course, decorrelating cruise from a
  repeatedly identical cursor-escape heading;
- existing variable speed, drift, later saccades, and local landing remain
  authoritative; and
- a bounded inward response operates only near a movement edge and pauses
  during an existing saccade.

There is no screen-region destination, opposite-side command, coverage memory,
food target, or territory-maximization objective.

## Anti-confinement evidence contract

The feature-gated trace records the current virtual-desktop movement bounds
with every sample. Metrics normalize completed Flight positions to those
bounds. The observed horizontal span and visited tertiles are diagnostic
outputs; neither feeds back into the motor.

In addition to all frozen duration, speed, path, saccade, landing, walking, and
grooming gates, the review requires at least ten complete flights from at least
twelve owner triggers. Its fail-closed software anti-confinement check requires
samples in at least two horizontal tertiles and at least one meaningfully
leftward and one meaningfully rightward completed bout. It does not require all
three tertiles, a center crossing, an opposite-side landing, or a minimum
screen-coverage fraction.

This check rejects a repeated motor lock like the R7 R4 observation without
claiming that a real fly should cover a prescribed fraction of an arena.

## Deferred food-search model

A later food-search behavior must be a separate behavior-layer experiment with
explicit hunger, food/odor evidence, and a global-to-local search transition.
It must not be inferred from display geometry inside the presentation motor.

## Primary evidence used for the boundary

- Tammero and Dickinson (2002), *The influence of visual landscape on the free
  flight behavior of the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster*:
  <https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.205.3.327>
- Elzinga, Iwasaki, and Dickinson (2015), *Body saccades of Drosophila consist
  of stereotyped banked turns*: <https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.114280>
- Corfas, Sharma, and Dickinson (2019), *Diverse Food-Sensing Neurons Trigger
  Idiothetic Local Search in Drosophila*:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2019.03.004>
- Whitehead et al. (2024 preprint), *Exploration-exploitation trade-off is
  regulated by metabolic state and taste value in Drosophila*:
  <https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.05.13.594045>

## Boundary

The experiment remains available only with the existing
`n41-visual-review-b` compile feature and `--n41-b-visual-review` runtime flag.
The supplied repository, DesktopFly, real AppData, shortcuts, and unrelated pet
processes are outside the write/control boundary. Promotion and deployment are
not authorized by a passing review.
