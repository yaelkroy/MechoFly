# N4.1-B natural-motion correction

## R2 rejection and sub-pixel root cause

The ten-minute N4.1-B visual review was correctly rejected. In the Windows
presentation loop, every display refresh read the layered window's integer
coordinates and wrote them back over `PetMotion`'s floating-point position.
At high refresh rates, one walking increment is often less than half a pixel.
Windows rounded that increment away before the next refresh, while the heading
continued to change. The visible result was a fly that pivoted and animated its
legs in place.

The fix accepts native coordinates only while the user is actively dragging.
Autonomous movement retains its sub-pixel accumulator. A 240 Hz regression test
round-trips the apparent native coordinates for two seconds and requires more
than 100 pixels of true translation.

## R3 rejection and clockwork-bout root cause

R3 fixed translation and added path motifs, but its ten-minute trace exposed a
second defect: every autonomous walk used exactly `56.060608 px/s`, and Walk
targets were still sampled uniformly from 60–150 frames. The 32 complete bouts
therefore lasted only 1.98–4.95 seconds, and duration predicted distance with
R-squared above 0.9999. That is an engineering clock, not credible stop-start
insect locomotion.

R4 does not edit any frozen N4.1-A/B/C file. It adds the versioned experimental
profile `n41-b-natural`, identified by parameter SHA-256
`a6c32576e4b869d10b8ff6f58ed3a7c9482ad831c767d697e5fd5e90e888ec6c`.
Its 128 event-keyed Walk quantiles span 3–313 model frames (0.099–10.329 s),
with many sub-second bouts and a sparse long tail. The authored table is a
50/50 mixture of exponential reference scales 1.37 and 2.10 seconds reported
for different zones by Valente et al.; it is a literature-shaped product prior,
not a fit and not a universal biological law.

Each Walk entry also selects one of 16 deterministic speed levels from
0.55–1.60 times the existing reference speed. A smooth 160 ms onset and two
low-amplitude within-bout oscillations prevent a single metronomic velocity.
Turn rate uses the same onset envelope, so heading cannot rotate while forward
translation is still effectively zero. No wall-clock random number generator is
used; replay remains event-key deterministic.

## Realistic behavior contract

This slice uses research as a qualitative movement constraint, not as a claim
that MechoFly is a calibrated biological model.

Walking fruit-fly trajectories are stop-start: motion-segment durations have a
large mass of short events and a sparse long tail, while smooth, often curved walking
segments are punctuated by stops and distinct sharp turns. The existing neural
controller continues to own the Walk/Quiet/Rest transitions. The natural-bout
profile selects the Walk target from its versioned quantile table. Inside each
controller-authorized Walk bout, the presentation layer deterministically
selects a straight, gently curved, or early sharp-turn motor motif. It never
creates a new macro behavior and never rotates without translating during a
walking sample. See [Mechanisms underlying attraction to odors in walking
*Drosophila*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105121/) and
[The structure of free-walking *Drosophila* trajectories](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0001083).

Fly grooming is a structured sequence of repeated, body-region-specific
actions rather than one generic leg twitch. Inside a controller-authorized
Groom bout, the authored motor program is:

| Substate | Frames | Modeled duration | Visible action |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Prepare | 8 | 264 ms | Forelegs rise |
| Head sweep | 30 | 990 ms | Alternating foreleg sweeps over head, eyes, antennae |
| Foreleg rub | 15 | 495 ms | Forelegs rub together near the mouth/head |
| Abdomen brush | 8 | 264 ms | Hind legs brush the abdomen |
| Wing clean | 8 | 264 ms | One hind leg reaches a visibly opened wing |
| Reset | 6 | 198 ms | Limbs return to stance |

The action ordering and approximate action scale are literature-inspired; the
exact durations are authored acceptance parameters. Sources: [A computational
analysis of grooming syntax](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007105/)
and [A suppression hierarchy among competing motor programs drives sequential
grooming in *Drosophila*](https://elifesciences.org/articles/02951).

## Evidence contract

The feature-gated review build emits a JSON-lines trace containing behavior,
position, heading, speed, hover/drag state, and grooming substate. Its images
are rendered directly from the pet's premultiplied BGRA buffer and composited
over RGB `(28, 24, 36)`. No desktop or application pixels behind the transparent
window are read.

The ten-minute gate requires at least 20 complete, autonomous Walk bouts;
duration CV at least 0.55; at least 25% of bouts no longer than one second; at
least one bout of 4.5 seconds or longer; per-bout mean-speed CV at least 0.18
with a 20 px/s range; duration-distance R-squared no greater than 0.98; at least
100 pixels of unhovered walking path; no more than 1% stationary-rotation
samples; at least one autonomous grooming bout of at least 1.5 seconds; and
direct images of head sweep, foreleg rub, abdomen brush, and wing cleaning.
Human acceptance separately asks whether a repeated clockwork rhythm is still
visible. Interaction-contaminated and right-censored Walk bouts are excluded.

After the final direct-buffer capture, the collector releases evidence hold,
closes only the process it launched, waits for the trace writer to release the
file, and only then calculates metrics. This repairs R3's file-sharing failure
without stopping any pre-existing pet process.

The exact application-seed, empty-input 600-second probe must also be repeated
for the selected runtime tier. This is an engineering regression and evidence
gate. It is not biological validation, promotion, or deployment authorization.

The added motion fields advance the canonical self-test receipt to schema 11.
The native checker, setup predicate, static integration verifier, and CI gate
all require that same schema so an additive receipt change cannot silently
invalidate the canonical verification path.

## R4 review-collector failure and repair

R4's Windows run reached the end of its 600-second review with a complete trace,
but `Add-CompletedWalkBout` could not bind its initially empty generic-list
destination. This was a PowerShell evidence-tool defect, not a controller or
motor failure. Independent replay of the uploaded trace passes all objective
gates: 81 autonomous complete Walk bouts, duration CV `0.932981`, speed CV
`0.364328`, duration-distance R-squared `0.806080`, `6791.600` walking pixels,
zero stationary-rotation pairs, four grooming bouts, and all required grooming
substates and captures.

The review-repair branch leaves every Rust file and parameter artifact
unchanged. Metrics parameters that intentionally support no observations now
carry `AllowEmptyCollection`, the objective analyzer can be replayed offline
with trace/capture hashes, and synthetic regressions exercise both zero Walk
bouts and the first completed Walk bout. Because R4 did not persist the late
human ratings or final owner decision, this objective recovery does not promote
the candidate and a fresh visual review is still required.

## R5 review closure

R5 re-ran the exact accepted-controller candidate from a clean identity and
completed the full review. The fresh trace contains 54 complete autonomous Walk
bouts, duration CV `0.987576`, speed CV `0.392977`, duration-distance R-squared
`0.909638`, `5699.533` walking pixels, zero stationary-rotation pairs, and four
structured grooming bouts. All six owner ratings and the exact-binary final
confirmation pass. R5 therefore closes the walking/grooming correction for the
next guarded step; it does not authorize deployment. The additive follow-on
flight experiment is specified in `docs/N4.1-Natural-Flight.md`.
