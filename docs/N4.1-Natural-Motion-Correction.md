# N4.1-B natural-motion correction

## Rejection and root cause

The ten-minute N4.1-B visual review was correctly rejected. In the Windows
presentation loop, every display refresh read the layered window's integer
coordinates and wrote them back over `PetMotion`'s floating-point position.
At high refresh rates, one walking increment is often less than half a pixel.
Windows rounded that increment away before the next refresh, while the heading
continued to change. The visible result was a fly that pivoted and animated its
legs in place.

The fix accepts native coordinates only while the user is actively dragging.
Autonomous movement retains its sub-pixel accumulator. A 240 Hz regression test
round-trips the apparent native coordinates for two seconds and requires more
than 100 pixels of true translation.

## Realistic behavior contract

This slice uses research as a qualitative movement constraint, not as a claim
that MechoFly is a calibrated biological model.

Walking fruit-fly trajectories are stop-start: smooth, often curved walking
segments are punctuated by stops and distinct sharp turns. The existing neural
controller continues to own the Walk/Quiet/Rest transitions. Inside each
controller-authorized Walk bout, the presentation layer deterministically
selects a straight, gently curved, or early sharp-turn motor motif. It never
creates a new macro behavior and never rotates without translating during a
walking sample. See [Mechanisms underlying attraction to odors in walking
*Drosophila*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7105121/) and
[The structure of free-walking *Drosophila* trajectories](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0001083).

Fly grooming is a structured sequence of repeated, body-region-specific
actions rather than one generic leg twitch. Inside a controller-authorized
Groom bout, the authored motor program is:

| Substate | Frames | Modeled duration | Visible action |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Prepare | 8 | 264 ms | Forelegs rise |
| Head sweep | 30 | 990 ms | Alternating foreleg sweeps over head, eyes, antennae |
| Foreleg rub | 15 | 495 ms | Forelegs rub together near the mouth/head |
| Abdomen brush | 8 | 264 ms | Hind legs brush the abdomen |
| Wing clean | 8 | 264 ms | One hind leg reaches a visibly opened wing |
| Reset | 6 | 198 ms | Limbs return to stance |

The action ordering and approximate action scale are literature-inspired; the
exact durations are authored acceptance parameters. Sources: [A computational
analysis of grooming syntax](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007105/)
and [A suppression hierarchy among competing motor programs drives sequential
grooming in *Drosophila*](https://elifesciences.org/articles/02951).

## Evidence contract

The feature-gated review build emits a JSON-lines trace containing behavior,
position, heading, speed, hover/drag state, and grooming substate. Its images
are rendered directly from the pet's premultiplied BGRA buffer and composited
over RGB `(28, 24, 36)`. No desktop or application pixels behind the transparent
window are read.

The ten-minute gate requires at least 100 pixels of unhovered walking path,
less than or equal to 5% stationary-rotation walking samples, at least one
autonomous grooming bout of at least 1.5 seconds, and direct images of head
sweep, foreleg rub, abdomen brush, and wing cleaning. Human acceptance still
requires visible naturalness and non-disruption.

The exact application-seed, empty-input 600-second probe must also be repeated
for the selected runtime tier. This is an engineering regression and evidence
gate. It is not biological validation, promotion, or deployment authorization.

The added motion fields advance the canonical self-test receipt to schema 11.
The native checker, setup predicate, static integration verifier, and CI gate
all require that same schema so an additive receipt change cannot silently
invalidate the canonical verification path.
