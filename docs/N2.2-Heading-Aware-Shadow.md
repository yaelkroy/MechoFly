# N2.2 Heading-Aware Shadow Footprint

N2.1 correctly moved the shadow center into screen/world coordinates and made
its distance, size, and opacity depend on Z altitude. It incorrectly made the
entire anisotropic ellipse heading-invariant, producing a permanently
horizontal oval.

## Correct contract

```text
shadow center:
  screen/world-space projection below the fly
  independent of fly heading

shadow footprint:
  anisotropic
  core and halo share one axis
  major axis follows fly heading modulo 180 degrees

Z behavior:
  greater altitude -> farther, smaller, lighter shadow
```

The axis is modulo 180 degrees because an ellipse has no directional arrow: a
fly heading and that heading plus π describe the same projected body axis.

## Claim boundary

This remains an authored orthographic projection under fixed virtual lighting.
It is not ray tracing, physical surface sensing, or reconstructed insect
biomechanics. No neural dynamics, controller threshold, fixed hold, transition
priority, telemetry, or landing-target rule is changed.
