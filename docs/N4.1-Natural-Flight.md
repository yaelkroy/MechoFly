# N4.1-C natural-flight foundation

## Entry decision

R5 recovered and completed the exact R4 natural-bout review. Its manifest and
all 251 declared files verify, every objective ground-motion and grooming gate
passes in both the fresh R5 run and the recovered R4 replay, all six early/late
owner ratings pass, and the owner explicitly accepted the binary for the next
guarded step. R5 remains a review candidate, not a deployed application.

The next visible clock is flight. The accepted profile still gives every Flight
bout exactly 121 model frames (`3.993 s`), one cruise speed, and a continuous
sinusoidal turn. This slice changes that feature-gated review surface only.
Canonical N4 and every previously named N4.1 parameter artifact remain
byte-identical.

## Qualitative realism contract

The motor structure is constrained by primary flight research, but its exact
numbers are authored product parameters rather than a biological fit.

- Voluntary and escape takeoffs are distinct motor strategies, so this slice
  keeps MechoFly's existing bounded looming trigger and already validated
  pre-escape timing instead of inventing spontaneous zero-input escapes. See
  [Card and Dickinson, 2008](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/211/3/341/18097/Performance-trade-offs-in-the-flight-initiation-of).
- Free flight contains comparatively straight segments punctuated by fast body
  saccades rather than continuous smooth circling. See [Frye et al.'s free-flight
  measurements](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/8z7sn-smr28).
- Landing is an approach and deceleration problem. MechoFly therefore carries
  incoming flight velocity into a short local landing trajectory and reaches
  zero velocity at touchdown. This is a qualitative constraint, not a claim to
  reproduce the full visually guided landing controller described by
  [van Breugel and Dickinson, 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22573757/).

## Additive parameter identity

The new profile is `n41-b-natural-flight`, backed by
`n4.1-soft-fatigue-b-natural-flight-v3.json` with SHA-256
`cb3cd2654dcd4fa9def34fb0145645f5d61b59c96c407669cf1e9dd4f12628ef`.
It copies the accepted R5 walking table and all controller values exactly, then
adds a 128-entry event-keyed Flight duration table spanning 18-242 frames
(`0.594-7.986 s`). Its authored distribution includes short flights and a sparse
long tail instead of a single four-second clock.

Every Flight entry deterministically selects one of 16 cruise-speed levels.
Within a bout, speed blends continuously from the existing escape speed into
cruise, receives a small non-periodic-looking texture, and follows a low-drift
straight segment interrupted by one guaranteed and one optional smooth rapid
saccade. Replay remains deterministic for a given event sequence.

The existing six-frame PreEscape and fifteen-frame Landing controller envelopes
are frozen. Natural landing uses a cubic Hermite path whose initial derivative
equals the incoming flight velocity and whose terminal derivative is zero. The
target remains local and bounded to the current desktop surface.

## Isolation boundary

The ordinary build still uses an empty default feature set, constructs canonical
N4, rejects the review flag, and leaves `PetMotion::natural_flight_motion` false.
The new controller profile and motor are reachable only when both existing
review controls are present:

1. compile with feature `n41-visual-review-b`;
2. launch with `--n41-b-visual-review` and an absolute receipt path.

The retained feature/flag spelling is deliberate: it avoids widening the
application interface while the experiment is still guarded. The labeled
candidate window and launch receipt identify the N4.1-D uncued exploratory
continuation and `n41-b-natural-flight`, so it cannot be confused with R5.

This slice adds no autonomous escape trigger. A reviewer supplies bounded loom
input with `Ctrl+Alt+L`; the neural controller still selects PreEscape, Flight,
and Landing. Deployment, shortcut changes, promotion, AppData writes, and pet
process control outside the process launched by the collector remain denied.

## Objective and owner acceptance

The ten-minute owner review retains every accepted R5 walking and grooming
gate. The owner triggers at least twelve separated flights and lets each full
takeoff-to-touchdown sequence finish. The objective flight gate requires:

- at least ten complete Flight-to-Landing bouts and three distinct durations;
- duration CV at least `0.25`;
- per-bout mean-speed CV at least `0.08` and speed range at least `30 px/s`;
- at least `500 px` of total complete-flight path and two detected rapid-turn
  episodes;
- an anti-confinement diagnostic with at least two horizontal tertiles and at
  least one leftward and one rightward complete bout, with no screen-coverage
  target or minimum-coverage threshold;
- the natural-flight feature flag on every measured flight; and
- direct pet-buffer captures of takeoff, early flight, maneuver, landing, and
  touchdown.

The collector excludes incomplete Flight bouts, analyzes the trace only after
closing its own candidate, captures no desktop pixels, and never writes outside
its Downloads workspace. Early and late owner ratings explicitly cover varied
flight length/speed/path, straight segments with quick turns, continuous
landing, retained walking/grooming naturalness, responsiveness, and
non-disruption. Passing still authorizes only another guarded step.
